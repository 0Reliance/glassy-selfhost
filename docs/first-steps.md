# Your first ten minutes

Glassy is running and you've chosen your password. It opens on an empty Notes page. Here is
a short tour that leaves you with a note, a document, a saved link and an assistant at work,
and shows you where each one went.

The left bar has every page. Hover over an icon to see its name.

Each step links to a short lesson at [learn.glassy.fyi](https://learn.glassy.fyi) if you want
to see it done.

## 1. Write a note

On **Notes**, click **Write a note…** at the top and type. Put a word or two in **Add tags**
underneath, like `garden`, so you can filter by it later. Click away and it's saved.

On **Today**, the capture box does the same in one line: `#garden` in the text becomes the tag.

Lesson: [Capture a thought in seconds](https://learn.glassy.fyi/lessons/capture/capture-a-thought)

## 2. Start a document

Notes are for short things. For anything longer, open **Documents** and start a new one. It has
headings, lists and pictures, and saves as you type.

Lesson: [Write long-form documents](https://learn.glassy.fyi/lessons/capture/write-documents)

## 3. Save a link

Open **Keep** and add a web address. It's kept with your notes and searched with them. The
Glassy Capture browser extension saves the page you're on in one click.

Lesson: [Save anything from the web](https://learn.glassy.fyi/lessons/capture/save-from-the-web)

## 4. Connect an assistant

This is what Glassy is for. Give an AI assistant (Claude, Codex, Cursor or any app that speaks
MCP) its own key, and it can read what you've saved and write beside you under its own name.

Settings → **Agent access (MCP)**: pick the app, give the assistant a name, choose **Create
key**, and paste what Glassy shows you into that app. [Agents in your Glassy](agents.md) has the
details for each app.

Then ask it something, like "Read my garden note and add what I've forgotten." Its note appears
on Notes, marked with its name.

Lesson: [Connect an assistant](https://learn.glassy.fyi/lessons/agents/connect-an-assistant)

## 5. Answer when it asks

When a choice is yours, an assistant asks instead of guessing. Its question waits on **Today**,
with the choices as buttons. Each says what happens if you pick it. Click one, and the
assistant carries on.

Lesson: [Agents ask, you decide](https://learn.glassy.fyi/lessons/agents/agents-ask-you-decide)

## 6. Find it again

Type in the search box at the top of any page, or press `⌘K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux)
to search everything. **Knowledge Base** searches your notes, documents, links, voice memos and
Obsidian vault together. With a model set up for search, it finds things by what you meant as
well as by the words: see [AI and search](ai.md).

Lesson: [Find anything, from anywhere](https://learn.glassy.fyi/lessons/find/find-anything)

## 7. Tidy up

Done with a note? Open its **⋮** menu and choose **Archive**. It leaves your pages but is
still searched, and **Archive** in the left bar brings it back.

Lesson: [Archive a note, and get it back](https://learn.glassy.fyi/lessons/find/archive-a-note)
